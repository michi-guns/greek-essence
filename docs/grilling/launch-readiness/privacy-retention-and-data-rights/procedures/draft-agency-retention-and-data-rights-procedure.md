# Draft Agency Retention and Data-Rights Procedure

## Status and Purpose

Draft review input for Giorgos and applicable qualified privacy or legal review.
It illustrates how Greek Essence could carry out the already accepted retention,
access, correction, and deletion boundaries. It is not legal advice, an approved
agency procedure, evidence of execution, or authorization to process production
data.

## Locked Boundaries This Draft Must Preserve

- Each accepted enquiry and its protected website audit history are retained for
  twelve months from durable acceptance, then deleted from the production database.
- Corresponding agency notification and follow-up email copies follow the same
  twelve-month deletion rule in the agency inbox.
- A visitor has no public account, request-history screen, or unverified
  self-service deletion route.
- Earlier access, correction, or deletion requests require a verified manual
  process.
- A deleted request may remain in protected off-provider backups for up to thirty
  additional days; any restore is isolated and reapplies relevant deletion
  safeguards before production use.
- Routine agency work occurs in the business inbox; it does not require direct
  production-database access.

## Draft Operating Steps

1. **Maintain the retention view.** Giorgos keeps a simple private agency record
   or calendar reminder for the recurring deletion review. It identifies only the
   minimum needed to find the relevant inbox conversations; it is not copied into
   the repository, screenshots, general logs, or an unapproved third-party tool.
2. **Perform routine inbox deletion.** At the agreed review point, Giorgos removes
   the eligible agency notification and follow-up email copies, including ordinary
   attachments containing enquiry information. He retains no separate parallel
   archive merely to bypass the twelve-month rule.
3. **Use the approved technical deletion path.** The production database deletion
   mechanism is implemented and evidenced separately. Giorgos does not use direct
   database access as a routine agency tool; he requests necessary technical action
   through the approved path when an earlier verified request needs it.
4. **Handle an earlier data-rights request cautiously.** Before disclosing,
   correcting, or deleting data, Giorgos follows the identity-verification and
   response approach approved by the agency and qualified reviewer. Until that
   verification is complete, the request is not treated as authority to reveal
   prior enquiry content or make irreversible changes.
5. **Record only safe closure evidence.** The agency keeps the minimum protected
   record needed to show that a request was handled, without recreating the deleted
   enquiry in a spreadsheet, inbox, or project document. The exact evidence and
   retention of that record require qualified review.
6. **Escalate technical or recovery work.** Dimitri handles only the approved
   technical action, recovery, or isolated restore boundary. He does not decide
   agency legal responses or business disclosure. A restore must reapply the
   twelve-month cutoff and every relevant verified earlier deletion before any
   production use.

## Review Needed Before Real Enquiries

Giorgos must approve or correct this draft against the agency's actual inbox and
staffing practice. Qualified privacy or legal review must validate the identity
verification, response, documentation, public wording, and any jurisdictional
requirements. Technical launch evidence must separately prove the implemented
delete and restore paths using synthetic or otherwise controlled data.
