# Project Grilling Glossary

Only project-level terms sharpened during the high-level grill belong here.
Feature-specific terms belong in that feature's workspace when needed.

## Language

**Public Preview Release**: The first publicly accessible Greek Essence release,
expected to receive real visitors and real enquiries. It is production-facing
despite the word “preview.”
_Avoid_: Private prototype, internal demo

**Booking Request**: A visitor's expression of interest in an eligible
Experience. It does not create or confirm a Booking.
_Avoid_: Booking, confirmed booking

**Editorial Content**: Public marketing and catalogue information managed by
the content platform, distinct from customer-specific or operational records.

**Transactional Data**: Customer-specific or operational records whose state
changes through business workflows, including consultation and booking
requests.
