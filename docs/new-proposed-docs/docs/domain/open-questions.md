# Open Questions

Record unresolved questions that affect multiple assumptions or do not yet
justify their own assumption file.

## Commercial workflow

- Can staff create bookings without quotes?
- How is customer acceptance recorded?
- Can an accepted quote be edited or versioned?
- Are deposits required?

## Supplier workflow

- Are suppliers confirmed manually?
- Does the agency track supplier payment deadlines?
- Are cancellation terms stored per supplier service?

## Pricing

- Are prices per person, room, group, or service?
- Are taxes included?
- Are commissions modeled explicitly?
- Can agents override catalog prices?

## Traveler data

- Which identity fields are legally and operationally required?
- Are passport documents stored, or only metadata?
- What retention rules apply?

Move a question into an assumption file when development depends on a temporary
answer.
