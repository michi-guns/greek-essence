# Greek Essence Project Actors

## Purpose and Authority

This file defines the people, organization, and responsibility terms used across
Greek Essence project discussions and documentation. Read it before interpreting
or assigning responsibility among the agency, its personnel, the developer,
product manager, or technical publisher.

Current operator instructions and accepted product or technical decisions remain
controlling. Named production access, delegated authority, account ownership,
and operational evidence still require Launch Readiness validation.

## Actors and Responsibilities

### Greek Essence

Greek Essence is the travel-agency business and public brand represented by the
website. Visitors discover its Destinations and Experiences and submit enquiries
to the agency.

Greek Essence is an organization, not a person. Actions and approvals attributed
to it must be performed by Giorgos or an appropriately authorized agency
delegate.

### Giorgos — agency owner and client representative

Giorgos owns Greek Essence and represents the agency in this project. He owns
agency-side business decisions and approval of public business content,
including agency facts, services, claims, prices, media use, and other commercial
meaning.

Giorgos may delegate work or approval responsibility to agency personnel. The
project does not manage the agency's internal hierarchy or choose its delegates,
but it must know that a person is authorized before relying on a material
approval. Giorgos remains responsible for agency-side delegation.

Prefer **Giorgos** when referring to the person and **Greek Essence** when
referring to the business or public brand. Avoid the ambiguous term **client**
when the distinction affects responsibility.

### Dimitri — developer, product manager, and project operator

Dimitri designs and implements the software, runs the requirements and decision
process, documents accepted outcomes, and coordinates technical delivery. These
roles do not make Dimitri the approver of Greek Essence's business claims.

Dimitri is the expected technical publisher for the Public Preview Release. The
final assignment, production Sanity access, permissions, account recovery, and
handoff must be confirmed through Launch Readiness before launch.

## Technical Publisher Boundary

The technical publisher enters or updates agency-approved content, checks the
technical preview and applicable publication requirements, and publishes it in
Sanity. Technical publication does not transfer responsibility for approving
agency facts, claims, prices, media rights, or other business content to the
publisher.

Exact Sanity roles and permissions belong to Foundation Design; named production
access and executable proof belong to Launch Readiness.
