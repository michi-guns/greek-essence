# Glossary

## Customer

The person or organization responsible for the commercial relationship.

## Traveler

A person receiving travel services. A traveler may differ from the customer.

## Offering

A catalog item that may be proposed or sold, such as accommodation, transfer,
excursion, ferry, flight, insurance, or a package.

## Quote

A commercial proposal containing travelers, services, dates, prices, and terms.

## Booking

The operational and commercial record of agreed travel services.

## Commercial snapshot

A stored copy of the relevant offering title, description, dates, prices,
taxes, discounts, currency, and terms at the time of the quote or booking.

## Supplier

An organization providing a travel service.

## Transactional truth

Business data whose correctness and history must be preserved, such as
bookings, payments, and accepted prices.

## Catalog content

Editable content used to present and configure travel offerings.

## Aggregate

A domain consistency boundary whose state changes through controlled behavior.

## Port

An interface used by application or domain code to request an external
capability without depending on its technical implementation.

## Adapter

An infrastructure implementation of a port, such as a Drizzle repository or
Sanity catalog client.
