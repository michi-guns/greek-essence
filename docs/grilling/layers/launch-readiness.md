# Launch Readiness Layer

## Purpose

Prove that the accepted product and foundation can be operated safely for the
public preview. This layer answers **whether the required people, approvals,
provider facts, procedures, and evidence are ready for launch**. It is not a
substitute for product or architecture design.

## Belongs Here

- named client, content, account, inbox, incident, and recovery owners;
- approved English and Greek copy, media rights, public claims, and catalogue data;
- legal, privacy, commercial, tax, or travel-industry review where applicable;
- provider terms, regions, agreements, quotas, sender authorization, and account
  control;
- environment configuration, migration, monitoring, backup, restore, rollback,
  accessibility, security, browser, performance, and end-to-end evidence;
- handover procedures, response expectations, escalation routes, and launch
  checklists.

A matter belongs here when it can be supplied, assigned, verified, or changed
without redefining product truth or a durable development foundation.

## Decision Escalation

Launch-readiness items normally use registers, checklists, owners, evidence, and
blocker states rather than grilling decision IDs. If validation reveals a real
product choice or architectural constraint, route it back to the owning layer
and resolve it there. Never weaken an accepted safeguard or invent a missing
business fact merely to mark a launch item complete.

## Inputs and Outputs

Inputs are accepted Product and Domain Truth and Foundation Design outcomes.
Outputs are verified launch evidence, named external validations, operational
handoff, and explicit blockers. These outputs may authorize launch only through
a later bounded release decision; they do not authorize implementation by
appearing in this layer.

The existing working [Public Preview Release Validation](../../working/work-items/prototype-rebaseline/PREVIEW_RELEASE_VALIDATION.md)
is evidence for the later classification review, not automatically the complete
or canonical launch-readiness register.
