# Catalogue Discovery Glossary

These terms sharpen the Catalogue Discovery feature decisions. Project-level
terms remain in [../GLOSSARY.md](../GLOSSARY.md).

**Requestable Experience**: A published Experience that an authorized editor
has explicitly marked as eligible to receive a Booking Request. Requestable does
not mean available, reserved, or confirmed.

**Indicative “from” price**: A client-approved starting price presented with its
currency, charging basis, key inclusions, review date, and an explicit warning
that the final price and terms require confirmation. It is not a live quote or
confirmed offer.

**Withdrawn Experience**: An Experience that was previously public and has been
unpublished. It is absent from catalogue discovery and cannot receive new
Booking Requests, while its former URL provides a generic recovery path without
exposing stale content.
