## Locked Project Context

- Home and About are required launch pages.
- The site helps visitors discover destinations and Experiences, then browse or
  ask the agency for help.
- A request is not a reservation or confirmed booking.
- Public claims, imagery, credentials, testimonials, prices, and relationships
  require example-led client approval.
- English/Greek ownership and the served markets still require client
  validation.

## Scope

Define the visitor promise, trust information, core page roles, shared calls to
action, content evidence, and language boundaries for Home and About.

Do not design the catalogue, form fields, content-management implementation, or
visual design system in this grill. Record dependencies for their later grills.

## Next Question

ID: D-001

Topic:
The first promise a visitor should understand on the Home page.

Prompt:
When a visitor first opens Greek Essence, what should the page help them
understand before asking them to take action?

Options:

1. (recommended): **Discover Greece with personal agency help available.** Lead
   with useful destination and Experience inspiration, explain that Greek
   Essence can help plan, and offer clear paths to browse or request a
   consultation. Do not imply live prices, availability, or confirmed booking.
2. **Lead with the catalogue.** Put destinations, tours, and activities first;
   explain the agency and consultation service after visitors start browsing.
3. **Lead with consultation.** Present Greek Essence mainly as a personal
   planning service and make the catalogue supporting evidence rather than the
   primary entry point.

Why this matters:
This promise controls the Home page structure and the visitor's expectations.
If it sounds like an online booking site, visitors may expect instant prices and
confirmation that the preview does not provide.
